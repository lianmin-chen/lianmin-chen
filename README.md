## 👤 个人简介 | Profile

你好！我是 **Lianmin Chen（Liam Chen）**，**华中科技大学** 计算机科学与技术学院博士研究生。<br />
*Hi! I'm **Lianmin Chen (Liam Chen)**, a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science and Technology at **Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)**.*

我的学习与研究聚焦在**机器学习**领域，特别关注：<br />
*My study and research focus on **machine learning**, with particular interest in:*

  *Zeroth-order optimization — black-box optimization methods*
